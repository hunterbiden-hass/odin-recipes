Fruit Salad Recipe

Prep:10 min|Serves:4

Ingredients

  • 1 pineapple
  • 2 apples
  • 2 oranges
  • 1 watermelom slice
  • 2 bananas
  • 1 tbsp honey(optional)
  • Juice of 1 lemon

Steps

  1. Wash all the fruits
  2. Peel and cut everything into bite sized cubes
  3. Mix in large bowl
  4. Add the lemon juice and honey,then toss gently
  5. Chill for 15 minutes then serve.

Tips: Add the bananas last so they do not turn mushy
